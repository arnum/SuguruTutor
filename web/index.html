Suguru and Sudoku

Two logic puzzles with difficulty measured as effort, guided hints and statistics.

SuguruRegions with the numbers 1 to n. Equal numbers must not touch, not even diagonally.
SudokuThe numbers 1 to 9 once in every row, column and box.

They are two separate apps. Open a game and add it to your home screen from there; each keeps its own games and statistics.
